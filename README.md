# My Favorite Anime

## Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World

Re:Zero is my favorite anime because it has an interesting story, exciting moments, and unexpected plot twists. I like how the main character faces many challenges and tries his best to save the people he cares about. **Subaru Natsuki** is my favorite character because he never gives up even after experiencing many hardships. I highly recommend this anime to people who enjoy fantasy, adventure, and psychological stories.
